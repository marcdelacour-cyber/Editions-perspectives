Judging in Dance

Understanding how we see, compare and decide — without telling anyone what to see.

An editorial collection for adjudicators, dancers, coaches, teachers and anyone wishing to better understand the human mechanisms involved in judging dance.

A book to understand. Workbooks to assimilate. A collection to deepen the reflection. An Assistant to continue the dialogue.

A coherent approach, without imposing a model

These publications are neither a rulebook, a federation reference framework nor a single method of judging. They offer points of reference for better understanding what really happens when a performance is observed, compared and assessed.

Understand the mechanisms

Limited attention, selective perception, fatigue, emotions, experience, preferences, cognitive biases and contextual influences.

Connect ideas with the field

Concrete situations, divergent readings, decisions under constraint and relationships with dancers, coaches and audiences.

Develop greater awareness

Identify personal reference points, distinguish observation from interpretation, explain choices and make constructive use of doubt.

Respect the diversity of practices

The proposed tools can be adapted to different disciplines, formats, levels and existing institutional frameworks.
