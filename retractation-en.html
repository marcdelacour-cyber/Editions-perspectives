At a glance

  • As a general rule, you have 14 days from receipt of the book to notify us of your withdrawal.
  • After notification, the book must be returned within 14 days.
  • The direct cost of return is borne by you.
  • A reduction in value resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to examine the book may be taken into account.
  • Reimbursement may be withheld until the book is received or until proof of return shipment is provided.
